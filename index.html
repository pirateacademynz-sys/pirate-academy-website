Our approach

The C.A.R.E. framework

A simple, research-informed model that guides every session — helping families, communities and schools thrive together online.

  • C
    Connect — build trust and resilience together.
  • A
    Ask — think ethically, without fear or pressure.
  • R
    Reflect — pause, notice feelings, and reflect.
  • E
    Empower — manage emotions and build real skills.
