Who we are

What Pirate Academy does

Pirate Academy is a family-, community- and school-centred education and awareness initiative that helps people take back what the digital world quietly takes. We're not here to alarm you — we're here to equip you.

Founded by a cybersecurity engineer turned digital-parenting educator, Pirate Academy turns real technical insight into clear, practical workshops and structured courses for parents, caregivers, educators and community groups — all built on our C.A.R.E. framework, grounded in research and designed for real families and real classrooms.

Our framework

C.A.R.E. for the digital era

A simple, research-informed model that guides every workshop and course we deliver — helping families, communities and schools thrive together online.

Our mission

To equip every family, community and school with the knowledge, tools and confidence to keep children safe online — without losing their wellbeing or their childhood.

Calm, practical, values-driven guidance. Let's sail it together. 💛
